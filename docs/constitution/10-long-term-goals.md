# Article 10 — Long-term Goals

1. Complete the phase roadmap: Language Design (Phase 2), Compiler v1 (Phase 3), Runtime (Phase 4), AI Native (Phase 5), SDK (Phase 6), Production (Phase 7).
2. Maintain an engineering knowledge base in which every significant decision is traceable from proposal to implementation.
3. Grow a contributor community operating under this constitution.
4. Preserve the ability to understand and modify any part of the system ten years after it was written.

Goals are reviewed at the close of each phase and amended through the standard amendment process.
