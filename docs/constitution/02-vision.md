# Article 2 — Vision

Frontier Creative OS aims to become a production-grade platform (Phase 7 of the project roadmap) that:

- executes programs through a specified language and a verified compiler pipeline;
- runs on a runtime designed alongside the language rather than retrofitted to it;
- treats intelligent systems, in whatever form they take, as first-class participants in the platform;
- offers SDKs built on stable, versioned interfaces;
- is sustained by contributors, reviewers, and maintainers operating under this constitution.
