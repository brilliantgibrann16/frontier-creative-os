## Context

RFC-0019 (merged 2026-08-17 via PR #44) framed the Stage 0 → Stage 1
capability-stage transition (S04#1.2) and the Stage 1 language-content
scope that P04 requires, presenting the admissible scope packages
neutrally and opening exactly four Maintainer decisions (D1–D4). Per
S04#1.2 a stage is never entered implicitly: entry is a Maintainer
decision travelling the RFC → ADR path. On 2026-08-18 the Maintainer
decided all four questions explicitly and in writing. This ADR is the
authoritative record (Article 7; L-8 / INV-8) and records the directive
without addition (CN-14).

## Decision

1. **D1 — Stage transition: Stage 1 is entered now.** The Stage 0 →
   Stage 1 transition required by S04#1.2 is decided through the
   recorded path (proposal RFC-0019 → this recording ADR). Wave 2
   opens. No deferral review point is created; RFC-0019's W-D option
   is not taken.

2. **D2 — Stage 1 content scope: W-B, the interchange kernel.** The
   Stage 1 scope is exactly the RFC-0019 W-B package: the W-A validity
   kernel — the Stage 1 artifact model (what an FSL definition
   artifact is), its validity conditions (four-category complete per
   S04#3.3 and Baseline §10), and the validation-outcome contract
   (accept/reject, with rejections citing violated clause IDs) — plus
   one concrete serialized interchange form for FSL artifacts. The
   interchange form's own design space (an existing boring format per
   GL-15/DP-28 versus a bespoke form) is **not** chosen here; it is a
   recorded sub-decision for the amendment cycle that drafts the
   clauses, exactly as RFC-0019 registers it.

3. **D3 — Type/data model: explicitly labeled deferred.** Stage 1
   includes no type/data model. The territory is explicitly labeled
   per the Baseline §10 labeling obligation rather than left silent;
   consistent with GL-2's recorded narrow-complete guidance. A future
   type/data model enters only through its own amendment-path
   decision.

4. **D4 — Determinism: required.** Stage 1 validation outcomes must be
   deterministic for identical input artifacts. Candidate requirement
   DR-7 (RFC-0019) is therefore in scope for Stage 1 clause drafting
   alongside the W-B rows DR-1–DR-6.

5. **Nothing else is decided.** No syntax or grammar (W-C territory
   stays out), no clause text, no interchange-format choice, no
   namespace token (ADR-0008), no versioning scheme (RFC-0009 window),
   no compatibility policy (RFC-0005 window), no conformance levels,
   and no change to any RFC-0019 class C or class D item. Execution-
   facing semantics beyond validation-evaluation remain deferred
   (Stage ≥ 2, ADR-0006 Decision 3).

## Consequences

- The stage transition is recorded; S04#1.2 is satisfied for Stage 1
  entry by this decision cycle. The twelve Stage 0 contract clauses
  and all ratified S04 prose are unmodified by this ADR (INV-18):
  Stage 1 clause content travels one or more subsequent S04 amendment
  cycles (proposal RFC → recording ADR — S04 §Sequence flows, step 2).
- Clause drafting scope for Wave 2 is fixed: DR-1–DR-6 plus DR-7.
  Conformance evidence for every row remains blocked on G-SPEC(S13),
  B-08, and G-CONF exactly as RFC-0019 maps it; CN-9 closure for
  ratified Stage 1 clauses follows the recorded-exception mechanism
  used by ADR-0011 decision 7 until the P12 judge exists.
- P04 remains gated. After Stage 1 clauses ratify, P04's "ratified
  clauses" prerequisite is met for the W-B scope; P04 entry
  additionally requires the ratified S05 contract (B-06, B-08), G-CI
  (P01.5 Maintainer console action), P11 evidence conventions, and the
  P12 judge. Nothing here marks P04 executable.
- The RFC register row for RFC-0019 flips to Accepted (ADR-0012) in
  this change; the RFC file keeps its submission-time `Status: In
  Review` header per the ADR-0004 convention.
- Program-register alignment (PROGRAM.md P04/Wave 2 wording;
  S04 charter §7 remaining-decisions row) travels a follow-up
  docs-alignment change per the PR #43 precedent, keeping this cycle's
  diff to the decision record itself.
- Per Article 6, the Maintainer's merge of this decision cycle's PR is
  the approving act for this record.
- The knowledge-mirror sync for this ADR follows the merged state
  (mirror-follows-repository; L-9 / INV-9).

### Judgment calls disclosed (Baseline §12)

- **Verbatim scope transcription:** the decision wording restates the
  RFC-0019 package and question definitions in substance without
  adding or removing any scope element; where RFC-0019 registered a
  sub-decision (the interchange-format design space), this ADR
  preserves it as undecided rather than resolving it.
- **D4 recording level:** the determinism requirement is recorded at
  the outcome level (deterministic validation outcomes for identical
  input artifacts, per Blueprint §13 property 4 framing); its clause
  form is drafting work for the amendment cycle.
- **Register-alignment split:** flipping the RFC register row is the
  mechanical consequence of acceptance and lands here; broader program
  registers land in a follow-up alignment change, mirroring the
  PR #43 precedent.
