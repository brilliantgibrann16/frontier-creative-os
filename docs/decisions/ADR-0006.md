# ADR-0006 — Language Identity: Definition Core with Staged Evolution (I-D)

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Decider:** Maintainer (Constitution Article 6)
**References:** RFC-0001, RFC-0012, RFC-0007, ADR-0004, ADR-0005,
`D01_DECISION_BRIEF.md` (PR #18), `D01_DECISION_DOSSIER.md` (PR #22,
currency note PR #27), Issue #7

## Context

D-01 (Decision Register) = U-1 (Blueprint §14) is the controlling
unknown of the project: what the Frontier Specification Language (FSL)
*is* — its purpose, its intended consumers, its success criteria, and
consequently which downstream subsystems exist at all. Risk R-1
(identity ambiguity) is rated Critical (RFC-0001 §Motivation).

RFC-0001 supplied the decision frame and deliberately recorded no
recommendation. ADR-0004 routed the runtime-scope unknown (U-14,
RFC-0012) into this acceptance act and made the RFC-0007 packaging
review mandatory at or after acceptance. Per Constitution Article 7,
Baseline L-8, and INV-8, no RFC resolves anything until its acceptance
is recorded as an ADR; this ADR is that record. Gate G-D01 (PROGRAM.md
§4) is the controlling gate this decision meets.

On 2026-08-10 the Maintainer selected from the recorded option space,
explicitly and in writing, the four couplings the dossier's checklist
(§18) requires. This ADR introduces no option outside the recorded
frame (CN-14) and no new choice of its own.

## Considered options

The complete recorded option space (RFC-0001 §Design Space, order
preserved; DEFER per RFC-0001 §Consequences and DP-15):

- I-A — Specification/definition language: programs are structured
  definitions (specs, contracts, artifacts) consumed by tools
- I-B — General-purpose programming language
- I-C — Domain-specific language for creative-systems orchestration
- I-D — Interchange/definition core (I-A) with staged evolution path
  toward executable semantics
- DEFER — recorded deferral with a review date

Coupled recorded spaces resolved in the same act: U-14 runtime scope
(S-A / S-B / S-C, RFC-0012) and the mandatory U-8 packaging review
(N-yes / N-no / re-deferral, RFC-0007 per ADR-0004).

## Decision

1. **Identity (U-1 / D-01): I-D** — the Frontier Specification Language
   is an interchange/definition core (I-A scope) with a staged
   evolution path toward executable semantics.

2. **Runtime scope (U-14): S-B** — a validation-evaluation runtime,
   resolved inside this act per the ADR-0004 routing of RFC-0012. This
   matches RFC-0012's recorded coherence for I-D. Baseline L-3 stands:
   the runtime's observable behavior is spec-defined and it holds zero
   defining authority. Per the RFC-0012 S-B row, the boundary between
   the validation-evaluation runtime and S13 conformance tooling must
   be drawn precisely in specification work.

3. **Staging discipline — ratified up front** (required by the RFC-0001
   I-D row). The discipline consists of exactly what the frame records:

   - Capability stages are explicit and ordered. Stage 0 is the
     definition/validation core scoped by this ADR (the I-D core with
     the S-B runtime).
   - Execution-facing semantics beyond Stage 0 are deferred without
     being foreclosed. Each stage transition is a future Maintainer
     decision that travels the RFC → ADR path; no stage is entered
     implicitly (CN-14).
   - The language versioning scheme, when decided in the RFC-0009
     window, must encode capability stages (RFC-0001 U-10 interaction).
   - The detailed stage model is specification content: S04 must encode
     the stage model, and execution-facing specifications remain
     blocked per stage until the corresponding transition is recorded.

4. **Packaging (U-8): N-no** — the mandatory RFC-0007 review is
   conducted in this act; no package-distribution subsystem exists.
   The interim N-defer disposition (ADR-0004) is superseded. S11
   retires via the Baseline §13 amendment path — supersession with a
   successor pointer, never deletion (S11 acceptance criteria;
   Blueprint §7) — as a queued follow-up. Program P10 does not exist.

5. **RFC-0001 open questions:**

   - **Q1 (first-class consumers): explicitly re-deferred.** Review
     point: the S04 language-definition charter, the first act of P03
     (DP-15 review point recorded).
   - **Q2 (success criteria at year 1 / year 5 / year 20): explicitly
     re-deferred.** Same recorded review point as Q1.
   - **Q3 (which controlled unknowns are decided here):** decided in
     this act — U-14 (S-B) and U-8 (N-no). Remaining under their own
     recorded rules — U-6 (interim P-D instability window stays in
     force) and U-10 (interim V-D, no language version before 1.0,
     stays in force; the eventual scheme must encode capability
     stages); U-5 conformance levels remain future work per ADR-0004's
     RFC-0004 row.
   - **Q4 (naming): the name Frontier Specification Language is
     retained.** Per RFC-0001 A-3 the name is not evidence and
     constrains nothing; the selected identity requires no renaming.

## Consequences

### Gate and blocker effects

- **G-D01 is met**: the identity decision exists and is recorded here.
  PROGRAM.md §4/§6 register text is updated by its own revision PR per
  the PROGRAM.md §7 change process; nothing updates automatically.
- **B-01** (D-01 undecided) — closed.
- **B-02** (U-14 unknown) — resolved: S-B.
- **B-03** (packaging deferral) — closed by the N-no decision.
- B-04 through B-11 survive as recorded (brief §10; dossier §9),
  including B-04/B-05 (interim P-D and V-D remain in force until their
  full decisions supersede them) and B-06 (interface forms remain
  UNKNOWN until each interface spec ratifies).

### Downstream effects

- **P03 Language Definition (Wave 1) becomes newly executable** — the
  only program made newly executable at once (DEPENDENCY_GRAPH
  topological order): first use of the RFC-0002 clause-ID scheme
  (N-B + N-C) and the RFC-0008 M-B machine-readable clause index;
  S04's blocked content sections unblock; `TODO(blocked-by:)` markers
  discharge progressively as specification work proceeds.
- **P05 exists with minimal scope** bounded by the S-B
  validation-evaluation runtime; S06 is scoped minimal now and grows
  only by recorded stage transitions.
- **P10 does not exist**; S11 → Deprecated via the queued Baseline §13
  amendment.
- **Decision windows open**: RFC-0005 (full compatibility policy — the
  identity now fixes who the consumers are, subject to the Q1
  re-deferral's review point) and RFC-0009 (versioning scheme, which
  must encode capability stages).
- Execution-facing programs and specifications remain blocked per
  stage; entering any later stage requires its own RFC → ADR decision.
