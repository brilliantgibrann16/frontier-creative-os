# ADR-0001 — Ratification of Constitution v1

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-02
Decision maker: Maintainer
Tracking: [Issue #8](https://github.com/brilliantgibrann16/frontier-creative-os/issues/8) · [PR #9](https://github.com/brilliantgibrann16/frontier-creative-os/pull/9)

## Context

Frontier Creative OS completed Phase 0 (Foundation) with a working compiler prototype and test suite but without ratified governance. Issue #8 required a constitution defining the project's mission, vision, values, principles, governance, decision-making process, compatibility policy, release policy, and long-term goals. The constitution was drafted under `docs/constitution/` on branch `docs/issue-8-constitution-v1`, revised through Principal Engineer review (commit `6d23aec`), and marked Ratified at version 1.0.0 (commit `974cc6c`).

## Decision

Constitution v1, version 1.0.0, is ratified. PR #9 was approved by the Maintainer and squash-merged into `main` as commit `daf4ffe19a21318774e8deabec68c415602d90bc` on 2026-08-02. The constitution — `docs/constitution/README.md` and Articles 1–10 — is effective as of that merge.

## Consequences

- The governance model (Article 6), decision lifecycle and quality gates (Article 7), compatibility policy (Article 8), and release policy (Article 9) are binding.
- No architectural or governance decision is binding until recorded as an ADR; routine engineering decisions do not require ADRs (Article 7).
- Direct commits to `main` are prohibited (Article 6). This ADR was committed directly to `main` by explicit Maintainer instruction as a one-time bootstrapping exception; future ADRs merge through pull requests.
- Decision records are maintained in both locations per the Maintainer decision of 2026-08-01: this repository under `docs/decisions/` (permanent historical archive) and the Notion ADR Log (engineering workspace). RFCs will live in `docs/rfc/` and the Notion RFC Index; specifications in `docs/specifications/` and the Notion Specification Index.
- The constitution is amended only through RFC, ADR, and an approved pull request merged into `main`.

## Alternatives considered

- Continue operating without ratified governance — rejected; decisions would remain informal and unauditable as the project grows beyond a single engineer.
- Defer ratification until Phase 1 completes — rejected; the remaining Phase 1 work (RFC and specification processes) depends on the constitution being in force.
