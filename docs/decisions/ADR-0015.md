# ADR-0015 — Ratification of the S13 Verification & Conformance Subsystem Specification

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-08-19 · **Author:** Maintainer · **Decider:** Maintainer (Constitution Article 6)
**Governing Ratifications:** S04 Stage 0 (ADR-0011), S04 Stage 1 W-B (ADR-0013), S05 Compiler Boundary (ADR-0014)
**Frame Dispositions:** RFC-0004 (L-A + C-A, accepted per ADR-0004), RFC-0008 (M-B, accepted per ADR-0004 / ADR-0010)

---

## 1. Context

Following the completion of the Stage 1 W-B compiler implementation program (**P04.0–P04.5**, PR #51–PR #55), the compiler subsystem possesses comprehensive spec-derived test evidence (`docs/program/P04_4_SPEC_DERIVED_TEST_EVIDENCE.md`) and a formal baseline submission to the P12 frame (`docs/program/P04_5_CONFORMANCE_SUBMISSION.md`).

However, per **Blueprint INV-16** and **S04#7.6**:
> *"The conformance suite, not any implementation, is the sole judge of conformance. No implementation, test suite, or maintainer opinion substitutes."*

Implementation test evidence produced under P04/P11 does not constitute official conformance certification. To establish the formal judgment machinery, fixture corpus requirements, harness boundaries, and self-certification claim regime, **RFC-0022** was proposed and merged into `main` (commit `025a043`).

This ADR serves as the authoritative governance record (Constitution Article 6, Article 7) ratifying the **S13 Verification & Conformance Subsystem Specification** and allocating permanent, frozen clause identifiers (`S13#1.1`–`S13#4.4`).

---

## 2. Decision

The Maintainer decides:

1. **Acceptance of RFC-0022:** RFC-0022 is formally accepted as the governing normative basis for the S13 Verification & Conformance subsystem.
2. **Ratification of S13 Specification:** `specs/S13-verification-conformance.md` is updated from `Status: In Review` to `Status: Ratified`.
3. **Allocation and Freeze of Clause Identifiers:** The fifteen normative clauses defined in RFC-0022 are allocated permanent, frozen identifiers:
   - `S13#1.1`: Sole judge authority (INV-16).
   - `S13#1.2`: Binary conformance model (`CONFORMING` vs `NON_CONFORMING`, RFC-0004 L-A).
   - `S13#1.3`: Clause totality (100% testable clause pass rate required).
   - `S13#2.1`: Black-box invocation boundary (L-4, Blueprint §3).
   - `S13#2.2`: Standard harness interface (consuming ratified S05 CLI/API).
   - `S13#2.3`: Deterministic harness execution (DP-28).
   - `S13#2.4`: Reproducible machine-readable test evaluation reporting.
   - `S13#3.1`: Clause-traced fixture corpus (CN-9 discipline).
   - `S13#3.2`: Dual-corpus requirement (mandatory positive and negative fixture pairs per testable clause).
   - `S13#3.3`: Fixture immutability and version-pinning to specification revisions.
   - `S13#3.4`: Synthetic independence (fixtures derived directly from specification prose).
   - `S13#4.1`: Evidence-backed self-certification claim regime (RFC-0004 C-A).
   - `S13#4.2`: Conformance claim structure and required metadata fields.
   - `S13#4.3`: Claim invalidation conditions (supersession, discovered divergence).
   - `S13#4.4`: Public, immutable claim registry.
4. **Machine-Readable Clause Sidecar:** The S13 clause index sidecar `specs/S13-verification-conformance.index.yaml` is instantiated conforming strictly to the ADR-0010 schema and verified by `tools/checks/check_clause_index.py`.
5. **Preservation of Explicit Non-Scope:** Graded conformance levels (core vs full), third-party certification authorities, runtime dynamic evaluation conformance, and quantitative performance benchmarks remain explicitly deferred per RFC-0004 / ADR-0004 and Blueprint §13.

---

## 3. Consequences & Program Impact

- **Gate Discharged:** Entry gate **`G-SPEC(S13)`** is officially **MET**.
- **Blocker Resolved:** Blocker **`B-08`** (Specifications unratified) is resolved with respect to S13.
- **P12 Unblocked:** Implementation package **`P12.2` (Conformance Test Harness)** is unblocked and authorized for execution on a dedicated branch (`tools/conformance/`).
- **G-CONF Pathway Opened:** Establishes the concrete, mechanical prerequisite sequence to close global gate **`G-CONF`** upon execution of P12.2–P12.4.
