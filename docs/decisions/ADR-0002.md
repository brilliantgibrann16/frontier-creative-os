# ADR-0002 — Adoption of the Architecture Document Set

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Decision maker:** Maintainer (Constitution Article 6)
**Related:** PR #10, merge commit `0cebadde`; Constitution Article 7; ADR-0001

## Context

PR #10 introduced three architecture documents under `docs/architecture/`
with `Status: Proposed`: the Architecture Baseline (normative), the Master
System Blueprint (descriptive), and the Universal Engineering Doctrine
(explanatory). Per Article 7, no architectural decision is binding until
recorded as an ADR. The Maintainer merged PR #10 into `main` on 2026-08-02
and directed that the documents be treated as the authoritative source of
truth. This ADR records that decision.

## Decision

1. `docs/architecture/baseline.md` is adopted as **Architecture Baseline
   v1** (version 1.0.0), the rank-2 normative document beneath the
   Constitution.
2. `docs/architecture/blueprint.md` is adopted as the **Master System
   Blueprint**, a descriptive companion with no normative authority.
3. `docs/architecture/doctrine.md` is adopted as the **Universal
   Engineering Doctrine**, an explanatory companion; its tagged
   constraints (CN-1…CN-32) take effect with this adoption.
4. Normative precedence: Constitution > Architecture Baseline > Blueprint
   > Doctrine.

## Consequences

- Dependency Law L-1…L-12 and Invariants INV-1…INV-20 are binding on all
  future engineering artifacts.
- The Baseline is amended only per its §13 (RFC → ADR → PR).
- All open decisions recorded as UNKNOWN (U-1…U-14, D-01 et al.) remain
  open; adoption of these documents resolves none of them.
