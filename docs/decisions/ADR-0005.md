# ADR-0005 — Specification Corpus Home: `/specs`

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Decider:** Maintainer (Constitution Article 6)
**References:** RFC-0003, ADR-0004, PR #13, Issue #7

## Context

RFC-0003 recommended `docs/specifications/` as the specification corpus
home (T-A), consistent with a recorded 2026-08-01 Maintainer decision.
On 2026-08-02 the Maintainer explicitly directed: “Create a
production-grade /specs directory,” and subsequently merged PR #13
placing the sixteen-subsystem specification corpus at `/specs`. That
instruction and merge operationally resolved U-3 (D-13) contrary to the
earlier location choice; per L-8 an operational resolution must be
recorded to bind. The conflict was flagged in the PR #13 description and
in `specs/README.md` at the time.

## Decision

The specification corpus home is **`/specs`**, at the repository root.
The location element of RFC-0003’s ratified taxonomy (T-A) is amended
accordingly; every other element of the ratified taxonomy stands
unchanged. This records the Maintainer’s 2026-08-02 instruction; it is
not a new choice.

## Consequences

- U-3 (D-13) is closed (jointly with ADR-0004’s acceptance of RFC-0003).
- The empty `specs/fcos/` scaffold predating the corpus is obsolete; its
  removal is ordinary repository hygiene carrying no decision authority
  (L-6) and may occur in any future hygiene change.
- References to `docs/specifications/` in previously merged documents
  (RFC-0003 text, roadmap commentary) are historical statements of the
  then-current recommendation and are not corrected retroactively; this
  ADR supersedes them for the location question.
- The repository index (root `README.md`) describes `/specs` as the
  specification corpus home.
