# RFC-0020 — S04 Stage 1 W-B Interchange-Kernel Clause Set (Amendment Proposal)

**Status:** In Review · **Date:** 2026-08-18 · **Resolves:** ADR-0012 follow-up 1 (the Wave 2 W-B clause-drafting cycle)
**Subsystem:** S4 · **Amendment proposal per S04 §Sequence flows, step 2. Nothing below binds by existing here: clause text becomes authoritative only when a recording ADR ratifies it into S04 (L-8 / INV-8; INV-18).**

## Summary

Propose the minimum coherent Stage 1 clause set for the W-B interchange
kernel that ADR-0012 decided: two artifact-model clauses, two validity
clauses, six validation-outcome clauses (including the required
determinism clause), and two interchange-form clauses — twelve drafted
clauses with N-C identifiers continuing the grandfathered `S04`
document identifier. The thirteenth surface, the concrete serialized
interchange form itself (reserved proposed identifier S04#8.3), is
deliberately not drafted: ADR-0012 records the format choice as a
sub-decision of this amendment cycle, no format is authoritative for
FSL artifacts anywhere on `main`, and selecting one silently would
decide governance by inference (CN-14). This RFC presents that decision
surface (F1) neutrally and drafts everything that does not depend on
it.

## Motivation

P04 requires ratified S04 language-content clauses plus the ratified
S05 boundary contract before compiler work may begin (PROGRAM.md §5;
the program dependency graph). ADR-0012 entered Stage 1 (D1), fixed the
Stage 1 scope to the W-B interchange kernel (D2), deferred the
type/data model (D3), required deterministic validation outcomes (D4),
and queued exactly one drafting follow-up: a proposal RFC drafting the
W-B Stage 1 clause set, travelling proposal RFC → recording ADR per
INV-18, "not begun without its own authorization." This RFC is that
proposal, begun under the Maintainer's explicit written directive of
2026-08-18 authorizing the RFC-0020 drafting cycle.

### Proposed clause text

- **S04#5.1 — Artifact unit.** Category: defined. A Stage 1 FSL
  artifact is a finite serialized unit of FSL definition content
  expressed in the ratified Stage 1 interchange form (S04#8.1). The
  artifact is the unit of validation: every Stage 1 validity condition
  and every validation outcome applies to exactly one artifact.
  Source: ADR-0006, Decision 1; ADR-0012, D2.

- **S04#5.2 — Stage 1 self-containment.** Category: defined. Whether a
  Stage 1 artifact is valid is decidable from the artifact's serialized
  content and the ratified S04 clauses alone. Artifacts do not
  reference other artifacts at Stage 1; composition and inter-artifact
  reference are explicitly labeled deferred territory, entering, if
  ever, through their own amendment cycle. Source: Baseline §10
  labeling obligation; GL-2 narrow-complete guidance; ADR-0012, item 5
  (nothing else is decided).

- **S04#6.1 — Structural validity.** Category: defined. An artifact is
  structurally valid if and only if it conforms to the ratified
  interchange-form clauses (S04#8.1–S04#8.3). Structural validity is
  mechanically decidable from the artifact alone; an artifact that is
  not structurally valid is invalid without further evaluation.
  Source: ADR-0012, D2; Doctrine CN-6.

- **S04#6.2 — Validity closure.** Category: defined. An artifact is
  valid if and only if it violates no ratified Stage 1 validity clause.
  Every Stage 1 validity condition is a ratified S04 clause; no other
  source may add, remove, or weaken a validity condition (S04#2.1).
  Territory not covered by a ratified clause is never implicitly
  defined: per S04#3.3, every behavior specified at Stage 1 carries
  exactly one of the four ratified categories, and deferred territory
  is labeled, never silent. Source: Baseline §10; CN-6; S04#2.1;
  S04#3.3.

- **S04#7.1 — Validation-outcome model.** Category: defined. Validating
  an artifact yields exactly one outcome: accepted or rejected. An
  artifact is accepted if and only if it is valid per S04#6.2;
  otherwise it is rejected. Source: ADR-0012, D2.

- **S04#7.2 — Violated-clause identification.** Category: defined.
  Every rejection identifies the violated ratified clause or clauses by
  clause identifier. The set of violated clause identifiers reported
  for an artifact is a function of the artifact and the ratified clause
  set. A rejection that cites no violated clause is itself defective.
  Source: INV-17 corollary; the RFC-0019 DR-4 row.

- **S04#7.3 — Outcome authority.** Category: defined. Validation
  outcomes report; they never rule. The ratified prose is
  authoritative: an outcome that contradicts the ratified clauses is
  defective, whichever tool produced it, and no validator — including
  the S-B validation-evaluation runtime — acquires defining authority
  through its outcomes. Source: Baseline L-2, L-3; AX-1; S04#1.3;
  S04#2.1.

- **S04#7.4 — Outcome determinism.** Category: defined. For identical
  artifact content validated against the same ratified S04 clause set,
  the validation outcome and the set of violated clause identifiers are
  identical across runs, implementations, and consumer classes.
  Source: ADR-0012, D4; Blueprint §13, property 4.

- **S04#7.5 — Diagnostic presentation.** Category: unspecified. The
  wording, ordering, rendering, and formatting of diagnostic messages
  beyond the S04#7.2 violated-clause-identifier set are unspecified at
  Stage 1. A binding diagnostics contract is S05 territory and enters,
  if ever, through S05's own ratification path — never through this
  clause. Source: Baseline §10 labeling obligation; B-06.

- **S04#7.6 — Validation/conformance boundary.** Category: defined. A
  Stage 1 validation outcome is evidence about one artifact; it is
  never a conformance judgment about an implementation. Conformance
  judgment belongs exclusively to the S13 conformance suite as the
  single implementation-independent judge; the S-B runtime evaluates
  artifacts and holds no judging authority. Source: ADR-0006,
  Decision 2 boundary obligation; INV-16; S04#1.3.

- **S04#8.1 — Single interchange form.** Category: defined. At Stage 1
  exactly one concrete serialized interchange form is ratified for FSL
  artifacts, and a Stage 1 artifact exists only in that form.
  Additional or alternative forms enter, if ever, through their own
  amendment cycle. Source: ADR-0012, D2.

- **S04#8.2 — Interchange-form obligations.** Category: defined. The
  ratified interchange form must satisfy all of the following, each
  compiled from a recorded decision: (a) it carries the S04#5.1
  artifact model; (b) well-formedness against the form is mechanically
  decidable, so that S04#6.1 structural validity and S04#7.4
  determinism are testable; (c) it is authorable and consumable by all
  three first-class consumer classes symmetrically (S04#2.2); (d) it
  embeds no type/data-model semantics — the deferral recorded by
  ADR-0012, D3 cannot be re-entered through the format. Source:
  ADR-0007, Decision Q1; ADR-0012, D2–D4.

- **S04#8.3 — Concrete interchange form.** RESERVED — no clause text is
  proposed. Drafting it before the recorded format sub-decision (F1
  below) would select the format silently (CN-14).

## F1 — Interchange-format decision surface (Maintainer decision; not made here)

ADR-0012 records the design space verbatim: "an existing boring format
per GL-15/DP-28 versus a bespoke form" — a sub-decision for this
amendment cycle. Live inspection for this proposal found no format that
is authoritative for FSL artifacts anywhere on `main`: the ADR-0010
YAML decision covers specification index sidecars (the M-B mechanism),
not FSL artifacts, and creates no artifact-serialization precedent.

- **F1-A — an existing boring standard format**, per the recorded
  GL-15/DP-28 guidance. Choosing the concrete format within this family
  is part of F1 itself.
- **F1-B — a bespoke FSL-specific form.**

Constraints on any choice: exactly the S04#8.2 obligations. Trade-offs
(analysis, not a choice): F1-A minimizes bespoke parsing surface and
tooling cost, consistent with the recorded boring-first guidance; F1-B
maximizes fit control but adds a parser and specification surface that
Stage 1 would then have to define, test, and maintain. Effect on P04:
the S05 input-acceptance boundary binds to the chosen form, so F1 sits
on the P04 critical path.

Admissible sequencing after F1 (either path satisfies INV-18):

1. **Revise-then-ratify:** the Maintainer decides F1 during this RFC's
   review; S04#8.3 clause text is added to this proposal by revision;
   one recording ADR ratifies all thirteen clauses.
2. **Ratify-twelve-then-follow-up:** the twelve drafted clauses ratify
   now; S04#8.3 travels an immediate follow-up amendment cycle after
   F1.

## Explicitly deferred and labeled territory

Per the Baseline §10 labeling obligation, the following is deferred,
not silent — none of it is proposed, and each enters, if ever, through
its own amendment or decision path:

- Type/data model (ADR-0012, D3 — the interchange form must not become
  one; S04#8.2 item d).
- Syntax and grammar (W-C territory; excluded by ADR-0012, D2).
- Composition and inter-artifact reference (S04#5.2).
- Execution-facing semantics beyond validation-evaluation (Stage ≥ 2;
  ADR-0006, Decision 3).
- Language versioning (RFC-0009 window) and compatibility policy
  (RFC-0005 window).
- Conformance levels (not decided — ADR-0012, item 5) and certification
  (G-CONF).
- Namespace tokens (ADR-0008 — a future token requires its own
  RFC → ADR cycle).
- Binding diagnostics taxonomy (S05 territory; B-06).
- Compiler, runtime, and any implementation architecture (P04 and
  beyond).

## Alternatives and trade-offs

- **Propose only the validity kernel without interchange-form
  obligations** — rejected: contradicts the decided D2 scope (W-B, not
  W-A).
- **Allow inter-artifact references at Stage 1** — rejected for this
  proposal: enlarges the validity surface into composition semantics
  with no recorded decision to compile from; deferred and labeled
  instead (S04#5.2; GL-2).
- **Fold diagnostic presentation into the determinism clause as an
  obligation** — rejected: would draft an S05 diagnostics contract
  prematurely (B-06); labeled unspecified instead (S04#7.5).
- **Draft S04#8.3 with a provisionally chosen format** — rejected:
  ADR-0012 explicitly reserves the choice; CN-14 forbids deciding it by
  drafting.

the P12 judge exists.

If this proposal is accepted and its clauses ratify (including S04#8.3
after F1):

- **Met:** P04's "ratified clauses" prerequisite for the W-B scope (the
  program dependency graph edge).
- **Unblocked for drafting (not ratification):** the S05
  input-acceptance boundary gains its missing upstream input — the
  ratified artifact model and interchange form; S05 drafting remains
  its own cycle (B-06).
- **Still blocking P04:** the ratified S05 boundary contract (B-06,
  B-08); G-CI (the P01.5 Maintainer console action); P11 spec-derived
  evidence conventions applied to Stage 1 clause identifiers; the P12
  conformance judge (G-SPEC(S13), G-CONF).

Nothing in this RFC marks P04 executable.

What the W-B contract exposes to S05, on recorded basis only: the
ratified interchange form as the input-acceptance target; the S04#7.1
outcome model and the S04#7.2 violated-clause obligation that S05
diagnostics must realize; the S04#7.4 determinism obligation over
validation outcomes. S05 is not modified by this proposal.

## Acceptance criteria

This proposal is correctly disposed when: (1) the Maintainer decides F1
and F2; (2) a recording ADR ratifies the reviewed clause text into a
Stage 1 clause-set section of `specs/S04-language-definition.md`
through the amendment path, allocating the proposed identifiers;
(3) the sidecar gains the matching entries (ADR-0010 schema) in the
same cycle and `tools/checks/check_clause_index.py` passes; (4) the RFC
register and derived corpus update mechanically. Until every step
completes, nothing here binds (L-8 / INV-8).

## Non-goals

No syntax. No grammar. No type/data model. No execution semantics. No
compiler or runtime implementation. No namespace token. No versioning
or compatibility policy. No conformance levels. No change to S05, S13,
or any ratified artifact. No CI governance change. No stage beyond
Stage 1.

## Recommendation (labeled, non-binding)

Accept the twelve drafted clauses as the minimum coherent W-B kernel.
On F1 this proposal recommends nothing: the format decision surface is
presented neutrally with its recorded option families.
