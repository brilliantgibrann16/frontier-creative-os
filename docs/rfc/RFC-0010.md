# RFC-0010 — Multi-Maintainer Governance Procedure

**Status:** In Review · **Date:** 2026-08-02 · **Resolves:** U-11
**Subsystem:** S1 Governance · **D-01-independent: decidable now**

## Summary

Decide the procedure by which FCOS transitions from sole-maintainer
governance (Article 6: "The current Maintainer is the repository owner")
to multi-party governance, before the transition is needed.

## Motivation

U-11. Constitution Article 6 names the current Maintainer but does not
define succession, delegation, or plurality. Doctrine OB-4 records that
governance debt is cheapest to pay before contributors arrive; the sole-
maintainer RFC-review problem (Phase 2 blocker J.4) already exists today:
the author and the approver of every RFC are the same person.

## Assumptions

- A-1: Any change to Article 6 is a constitutional amendment and follows
  the constitution's own amendment rule (merge-effective, per Article 1
  amendment provision).
- A-2: This RFC frames the amendment; it cannot itself alter the
  constitution (rank 4 vs rank 1).

## Design Space

| # | Model | Trade-offs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G-A | Sole maintainer + named successor (bus-factor fix only) | Minimal change; solves succession, not review independence (J.4) |
| G-B | Maintainer + reviewers: delegated review authority, decision authority retained | Solves J.4 partially (independent review exists when reviewers exist); low machinery |
| G-C | Core team with quorum rules for ADR acceptance by artifact rank | Full plurality; heaviest machinery; premature at one contributor but the frame can be pre-ratified dormant |
| G-D | Time/contribution-triggered escalation: G-A now, auto-review point at Nth external contributor | Encodes the transition as governance, not crisis response (DP-15) |

Interim J.4 mitigation decidable under every model: mandatory cooling
period between RFC submission and self-acceptance, with written
self-review recorded in the ADR.

## Open Questions

1. What triggers the transition review — calendar, contributor count, or
   Maintainer discretion?
2. Do AI systems' first-class participation (Article 2) extend to review
   roles? The Article-2 interpretation flag (participation ≠ authority)
   remains open and gates this question.
3. Where is the successor named (constitution vs sealed designation)?

## Consequences

Entirely decidable now; no D-01 dependency. Deciding G-D + the interim
cooling rule removes blocker J.4 from the RFC-0001 critical path.

## Recommendation (non-binding)

G-D with the interim cooling-period rule adopted immediately.
