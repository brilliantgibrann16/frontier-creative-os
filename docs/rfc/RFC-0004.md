# RFC-0004 — Conformance Levels and Third-Party Certification

**Status:** In Review · **Date:** 2026-08-02 · **Resolves:** U-5 (D-04); U-13
**Subsystem:** S13 Verification & Conformance · **Partially D-01-dependent**

## Summary

Decide (a) whether conformance is binary or leveled (core/full, profiles,
versioned tiers), and (b) how third-party implementations obtain and
retain a recognized conformance status.

## Motivation

Baseline §10 fixes the conformance *model* (clause-traced suite, single
implementation-independent judge, INV-16) but deliberately not the
*levels*. U-13 becomes real the day a third-party implementation exists;
deciding the frame early prevents ad-hoc precedent.

## Assumptions

- A-1: Whatever the levels, the judge remains the single conformance
  suite (INV-16); levels partition clauses, never fork the suite.
- A-2: Certification asserts only what ratified specs cover (Blueprint
  §9, FCOS ↔ External world boundary).

## Design Space

**Levels:** (L-A) binary conform/not; (L-B) core + optional profiles;
(L-C) versioned tiers tied to language versions (U-10 interaction).
Trade-offs: L-A is simplest and strongest against dialects (P-2) but may
block legitimate constrained targets; L-B admits partial targets at the
cost of a profile registry and combinatorial testing; L-C couples to the
unresolved versioning scheme.

**Certification:** (C-A) self-certification against the published suite
with published results; (C-B) Maintainer-issued certification upon
submitted evidence; (C-C) delegated certifiers under ratified procedure.
Trade-offs: C-A scales with zero governance cost but relies on claim
honesty; C-B is authoritative but sole-maintainer-bottlenecked; C-C
requires the multi-party governance machinery of RFC-0010.

## Open Questions

1. Which clause categories (implementation-defined / unspecified /
   undefined) may a profile exclude, if any?
2. Does certification expire per language version (interacts with U-10,
   U-6)?
3. What mark/claim language may a certified implementation use?

## Consequences

The *frame* (this RFC) is decidable now; concrete profiles cannot be
enumerated before RFC-0001 and the first ratified specifications.

## Recommendation (non-binding)

Decide the frame now as L-A-until-superseded + C-A with mandatory
published evidence; revisit levels at first ratified specification.
