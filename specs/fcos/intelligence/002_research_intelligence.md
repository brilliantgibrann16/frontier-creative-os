# Research Intelligence

## Purpose

Research Intelligence is responsible for acquiring, validating, and synthesizing external knowledge before significant implementation decisions are made.

The objective is not to copy existing work.

The objective is to improve decision quality through evidence-based understanding.

---

# Cognitive Objective

Research Intelligence SHALL answer:

- What already exists?
- What represents current best practice?
- Which patterns consistently succeed?
- Which mistakes should be avoided?
- Which constraints influence this domain?
- Which opportunities remain unexplored?

---

# Research Sources

Sources MAY include:

- Official documentation
- Technical specifications
- Design systems
- Academic publications
- Engineering blogs
- Browser standards
- Framework documentation

Community discussions MAY be used for supplementary insight but SHALL NOT override authoritative sources without justification.

---

# Research Pipeline

Research SHALL execute the following stages.

## Stage 1 — Objective Definition

Identify research objectives.

Determine:

- unknowns
- assumptions
- required evidence

---

## Stage 2 — Source Collection

Gather relevant material.

Record:

- source
- publication date
- authority
- scope
- relevance

---

## Stage 3 — Evidence Validation

Evaluate:

- credibility
- recency
- consistency
- applicability

Conflicting evidence SHALL be documented rather than ignored.

---

## Stage 4 — Pattern Extraction

Extract recurring observations.

Examples:

- navigation patterns
- motion principles
- interaction conventions
- accessibility techniques
- performance optimizations

Patterns SHALL describe principles rather than implementations.

---

## Stage 5 — Opportunity Identification

Identify:

- innovation opportunities
- quality improvements
- unexplored approaches
- competitive advantages

Opportunity identification SHOULD prioritize originality over imitation.

---

# Evidence Matrix

Each significant conclusion SHALL include:

- supporting evidence
- confidence level
- affected subsystem
- implementation relevance

Confidence Levels:

High

Medium

Low

Unknown

---

# Failure Conditions

Research SHALL fail when:

- evidence is insufficient,
- sources cannot be validated,
- conclusions rely primarily on assumptions,
- conflicting information cannot be reconciled.

Failure SHALL trigger additional investigation.

---

# Quality Criteria

Research is considered complete when:

- relevant domains have been investigated,
- evidence has been validated,
- opportunities have been documented,
- assumptions are explicitly identified,
- conclusions are traceable.

---

---

# Guiding Statement

Research exists to expand understanding rather than justify preconceived solutions.

The value of research is measured by the quality of decisions it enables.
